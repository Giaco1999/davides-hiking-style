Design choices

Trails

  • Red is used only for marked trails.
  • One red, two dash patterns: dashed for marked trails, dotted for alpine routes (OSM sac_scale T4–T6).
  • Via ferrata: dense red dots with a carabiner icon every few dozen pixels.
  • National long-distance trails: thin red line z7–10, pale band under the trail from z11.

Roads and lines

  • Roads: motorways pink, state roads orange, the rest pale and narrow.
  • Forest roads: double line with a tan centre from z12, drawn under the roads. Unmarked paths: brown dash.
  • Railways: solid black. Tunnels: pale fill with dashed black edges.

Relief

  • Hillshade lit from the north-west, lighter at small scales.
  • Contours: index lines heavier, every 100 m from z14; blue on glaciers.

Areas

  • Woods from z5 (Planet v4 below z8).
  • Built-up tint from z7; buildings merged into masses z12–14, footprints from z14.
  • Protected areas from z9: purple edge, translucent inner band, name along the boundary. Natura 2000 sites excluded.

Labels

  • Lakes: one horizontal name per lake, sized by area, highest priority.
  • Peaks: main ones from z8, all from z12; names win over trails.
  • Hut and bivouac names avoid trails, using invisible collision symbols along them.
  • Trail numbers and river names sit above their line.
